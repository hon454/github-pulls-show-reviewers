## v1.17.1

Clearer options-page controls and more predictable sign-in feedback for
**GitHub Pulls Show Reviewers**.

### Improvements

- Improve primary-button text contrast, including hover states, and keep
  enabled actions in invalidated account cards fully opaque.
- Show localized success or manual-copy guidance after copying a sign-in code.
  Disable Copy while the clipboard operation is pending and discard stale
  feedback when the sign-in attempt changes.
- Focus the sign-in panel when opened or retried, restore a useful control when
  an internal transition removes focus, and preserve deliberate focus elsewhere.
- Keep a localized connection-complete status in the accounts section after
  the sign-in panel closes.

### Compatibility

- Existing accounts, display preferences, reviewer-state semantics, extension
  permissions and GitHub App access requirements are unchanged.
- English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese remain
  supported. Public repositories retain no-token access, subject to GitHub limits.
